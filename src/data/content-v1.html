About

Oselu Irabor

I’m Oselu Irabor, a visual artist, illustrator, designer, and art educator. My work spans illustration, visual storytelling, and design, driven by a curiosity about how images communicate ideas and how creativity shapes the way we see the world.

Alongside my creative practice, I design playful, hands-on learning experiences that help people explore their creativity through drawing, experimentation, and collaboration. I’m interested in making art more accessible, helping people build creative confidence, and creating spaces where curiosity matters more than getting things right.

Whether I’m making images or bringing people together to make things, my work is rooted in the same belief: creativity is something we can all explore, practise, and grow into.

How a Wonderful Labs class works

  • No experience needed. We start with simple sketching exercises that anyone can do.
  • Small steps. Each exercise builds on the last, so confidence grows before the hard parts arrive.
  • Real materials. Everyone works with proper drawing tools and oil pastels.
  • You leave with your own work. Every participant goes home with something they made.

Case study

From first lines to oil pastel: the Lagos class

A paid class in Lagos where twelve participants moved from simple sketching exercises to their own drawings and oil pastel paintings.

Where
Lagos
When
October 2026
Participants
12
Medium
Pencil & oil pastel
The brief The approach What they made What I learned

The brief

Introduce a group of twelve people to drawing and help each of them make their own work, starting from simple exercises rather than a blank page.

The approach

Instead of starting with a blank page and a big subject, the class moved in stages.

  1. Warm-up sketchingSimple, low-pressure exercises to loosen the hand and get past the fear of the first mark.
  2. Easing inBuilding from those exercises toward looking closely and drawing a real subject.
  3. DrawingEach participant developed their own drawing.
  4. Painting with oil pastelAdding colour, layering and blending with oil pastels to finish the piece.

What they made

Participants left with their drawings and oil pastel paintings.
